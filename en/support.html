Help and contact

Does the app tell me how much to water?

No. You choose how often to check each plant. When you log watering, the app suggests the next check and you can adjust it.

What is the difference between “Checked, no water” and “Postpone”?

“Checked, no water” logs a check and suggests coming back tomorrow. “Postpone” only moves the next check date, without logging anything.

I don’t get reminders

Make sure notifications are allowed in iOS Settings › Plant Journal › Notifications. In the app, Settings › Reminders shows how far ahead they are prepared; open the app now and then to renew them.

How do I move my data to another iPhone?

Settings › “Export backup” creates a .bitacora file. Save it in Files or send it with AirDrop, then use “Import backup” on the other iPhone. Importing replaces the library; it doesn’t merge.

I bought Pro and it doesn’t show

Use Settings › “Restore purchases” with the same App Store account you bought it with.
