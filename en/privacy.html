Privacy policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026.

Summary

Plant Journal does not collect personal data. There are no accounts, servers, ads, analytics or tracking.

Data you keep in the app

Plants, locations, notes, care dates, photos and preferences are stored only on your iPhone. They are not sent to us or to third parties.

Photos and camera

The app asks for camera access only when you choose “Take photo”. To pick photos from your library it uses the system picker, which only shares the photos you select. Photos are stored re-encoded and without location metadata.

Reminders

Reminders are local notifications scheduled and shown by your iPhone. They don’t go through any server.

Backups

Backups are files you create and keep wherever you choose (for example, Files or iCloud Drive). How they are handled afterwards depends on the service you pick. Deleting data in the app doesn’t delete backups you have already shared.

Purchases

Plant Journal Pro is processed by Apple through the App Store. We don’t receive your payment details.

Deleting your data

Settings › “Delete all data” removes the whole library from your iPhone. You can also delete the app.
